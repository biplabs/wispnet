Two ways to bring a service closer

Your circle. Or a wider one.

Keep a service strictly between your own paired devices, or choose to give it a public HTTPS address. You decide what is available and who gets in.

01 PRIVATE DEVICE ACCESS

Pair your devices.
Keep it personal.

Reach the services on your computers from another computer or your Android phone. Pair devices you trust via QR or invitation code, then choose which services each device can open.

  • End-to-end encrypted connections between your devices
  • Service permissions stay on the hosting computer
  • Open web apps and native shells directly from Wisp Space

No Wispnet account required

02 PUBLIC SERVICE ACCESS

Share a link.
Your service sets access.

Publish an HTTPS URL for a service you select. The service decides whether to require a login, API key, or no authentication.

  • Publish selected HTTP and MCP services explicitly
  • Keep authentication and user permissions in the service
  • Stop publishing from the hosting computer

Authentication belongs to each service
