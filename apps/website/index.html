Two ways to bring a service closer

Your circle. Or a wider one.

Keep a service between your own devices, or choose to give it a public address. You decide what’s available and who gets in.

01PRIVATE DEVICE ACCESS

Pair your devices.
Keep it personal.

Reach the services on your computers from another computer or your Android phone. Pair devices you trust, then choose which services each can open.

  • Encrypted connections between your devices
  • Permissions stay with the hosting computer
  • Open web services in your normal browser

✓ No Wispnet account required

02PUBLIC SERVICE ACCESS

Share a link.
Your service sets access.

Publish an HTTPS URL for a service you select. The service decides whether to require a login, API key, or no authentication.

  • Publish selected services explicitly
  • Keep authentication and user permissions in the service
  • Stop publishing from the hosting computer

↗ Authentication belongs to each service
